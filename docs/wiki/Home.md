# wagtail-kanban-content-calendar

A kanban board and content calendar for Wagtail pages, inside the Wagtail admin. Every card is a real page and
every move is one of Wagtail's own actions, recorded in the page's history.

![Dragging a card, opening its quick view, setting a target date](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JR91387/wagtail-kanban-content-calendar/main/docs/images/demo.gif)
